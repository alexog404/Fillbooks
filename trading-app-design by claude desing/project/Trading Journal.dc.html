Where the themes diverge
Win/loss hues are NOT a token swap: dark uses saturated #22c55e / #ef4444 (near-black backdrop lets neon read clean); light uses darkened #0f7a3d / #c0271f — the dark pair goes illegible-neon on white. Elevation also diverges: dark relies on surface lightness only (no shadow), light adds hairline borders + a 1px shadow since surfaces are near-white.
Contrast (WCAG AA)
Dark: text-primary on bg ≈ 15:1, win/loss on bg ≈ 8–9:1 — passes AA comfortably.
Light: text-primary on bg ≈ 16:1, win #0f7a3d on white ≈ 5.8:1, loss #c0271f on white ≈ 5.2:1 — both pass AA (4.5:1) for normal text.
Caution: text-muted in dark (#6b7386 on #0a0e16) ≈ 4.6:1 — fine for body text, avoid for anything below 12px.
